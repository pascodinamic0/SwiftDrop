# Kitchen to door tracking gaps: where Kinshasa delivery promises die

The kitchen finished. The rider is coming. The customer calls. The merchant calls. Nobody shares one status. Tracking gaps turn delivery into a rumour network you already pay for in support time.

## Definition

A **kitchen-to-door tracking gap** is any stage of an order without a durable status customers and staff can see without calling.

## What doing nothing already costs

| Leak | What you already pay |
|------|----------------------|
| No status | Anxiety calls |
| Stale status | False ETAs |
| Chat-only updates | No audit |
| Merchant blind | Overprep or underprep |

Escape, not shame: people running notebooks and chats were holding things together. Give them a calmer system.

## Framework

1. Define statuses: accepted, preparing, ready, assigned, picked up, in transit, delivered
2. Update in under 30 seconds from phone
3. Show customer a calm timeline
4. Alert merchants on exceptions
5. Keep WhatsApp as notify, not database

## Believable operational scenario

A Gombe restaurant marks ready on a whiteboard. The rider lives in a group chat. The customer refreshes nothing useful. Two calls later the food is cold.

## Common mistakes

- Statuses only on paper
- Riders updating hours later
- Fake ETAs to silence callers



## Tools that help

Live tracking, merchant console, rider assignment status, customer timeline.


## Checklist

- [ ] Loss written as one dated sentence from the last 30 days
- [ ] Business object and ID defined
- [ ] Capture role and review role named
- [ ] Parallel run for two weeks
- [ ] Success measured in time, disputes, or gaps
- [ ] Shadow process retired for pilot scope only

## Fourteen-day path

Days 1-2: date the loss. Days 3-4: define the object. Days 5-7: capture new events on the object. Days 8-10: compare disputes and time-to-answer. Days 11-14: retire the shadow process for the pilot scope. If calm does not improve, simplify before blaming staff.

## Why this leak compounds weekly

Every week you postpone the object, improvisations multiply. New staff learn the workaround. Leadership sees calm until a dispute forces reconstruction. Date one incident, then the next.

## What good looks like after two weeks

Capture feels faster than the old habit. Review happens during the week. Leave does not trap the only truth. Leadership answers the top question without calling three people.

## Teaching adoption without a revolt

Stand beside the person who carries the process. Ask what would make capture easier than their workaround. Build the pilot around that answer. Celebrate matched days between old and new records.

## Metrics that matter

Matter: time to answer, disputes, age of exceptions, length of close or report build. Ignore: raw logins, messages sent, features enabled.

## Kinshasa / Nairobi operating notes

Expect mobile money, dual currencies where relevant, WhatsApp as front door, patchy signal. Localise language and payment labels. Keep object discipline identical across cities.

## How AI changes this (honestly)

AI can remind, summarise, translate, and flag anomalies. It cannot invent proof you never captured. Design objects and evidence rules first.
