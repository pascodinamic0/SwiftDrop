# COD delivery fee clarity: why opaque fees kill Kinshasa orders

Customers prepay items if the journey feels fair. They abandon when the delivery fee is a surprise at the door. Merchants eat prep cost. Riders argue. Opaque COD fees are a trust leak you already fund with cancellations.

## Definition

**COD delivery fee clarity** means the customer knows before confirming how the delivery fee will be paid (often cash on arrival) and roughly what it will be for their zone — while items may be prepaid.

## What doing nothing already costs

| Leak | What you already pay |
|------|----------------------|
| Surprise fee | Cancel at the door |
| Zone confusion | Rider arguments |
| Merchant prep waste | Food and margin lost |
| Support chats | Evenings explaining |

Escape, not shame: people running notebooks and chats were holding things together. Give them a calmer system.

## Framework

1. Publish zone fee rules in plain language
2. Show fee estimate before confirm
3. Separate item prepay from cash fee on arrival
4. Log exceptions with reason codes
5. Train riders not to renegotiate privately

## Believable operational scenario

A pharmacy order from Lingwala prepays medicines. At the door the fee is higher than the chat implied. The customer refuses. Medicines return. The next customer never trusts the channel.

## Common mistakes

- Hiding fees until arrival
- Different fees per rider mood
- No zone map
- Mixing item balance with fee in one vague number

## Prepay vs fee

Prepaying items protects merchants. Cash fees on arrival match rider and customer habits. Confusion starts when those two amounts are mashed into one unclear promise.

## Tools that help

Checkout that separates item total and delivery fee; zone table; rider script; exception codes.

## Why this leak compounds weekly

Every week you postpone the object, improvisations multiply. New staff learn the workaround. Leadership sees calm until a dispute forces reconstruction. Date one incident, then the next.

## What good looks like after two weeks

Capture feels faster than the old habit. Review happens during the week. Leave does not trap the only truth. Leadership answers the top question without calling three people.

## Teaching adoption without a revolt

Stand beside the person who carries the process. Ask what would make capture easier than their workaround. Build the pilot around that answer. Celebrate matched days between old and new records.

## Metrics that matter

Matter: time to answer, disputes, age of exceptions, length of close or report build. Ignore: raw logins, messages sent, features enabled.

## Kinshasa / Nairobi operating notes

Expect mobile money, dual currencies where relevant, WhatsApp as front door, patchy signal. Localise language and payment labels. Keep object discipline identical across cities.

## How AI changes this (honestly)

AI can remind, summarise, translate, and flag anomalies. It cannot invent proof you never captured. Design objects and evidence rules first.


## FAQ

**Why cash fee on arrival?**

It matches how many Kinshasa customers settle the ride while prepaid items reduce merchant no-show risk.

**Can fees be free?**

Sometimes as promotion — still state the rule.
